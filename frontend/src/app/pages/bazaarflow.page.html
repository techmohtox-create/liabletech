Built for Indian commerce

The rules that cost money are built in, and tested

Generic store builders leave GST, cash on delivery and stock accuracy to plugins and spreadsheets. In BazaarFlow they are part of the core, with automated tests on every rule that touches money.

  • GST on every lineCGST/SGST or IGST chosen by place of supply, with invoices numbered per financial year.
  • Cash on deliveryHandling fees, order limits and collection tracked through to dispatch.
  • Stock you can trustReserved at checkout, consumed at dispatch, every movement in an append-only ledger.
  • Returns and refundsCancellations, returns, credit notes and refunds without breaking your books.
